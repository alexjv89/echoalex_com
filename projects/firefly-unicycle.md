A Makeystreet-led community build at Jaaga in Bangalore. We built an open-source motor driver
and balance firmware, a dirt test track at Jaaga farm, and did a unicycle flash mob. The road
test at Sony World Signal in Koramangala is the most-watched video on my channel.

The maker years (2010 – 2015)

This used to be my LinkedIn About, written around 2012, right out of college. It still says something true about me, so it lives here now.

I would call myself a creative person. I like the process of making things. I like making things. I am a maker. If you are wondering why am I kind of repeating stuff, well I am trying to make a point.

I think making is an important thing. Its makers who become problem solvers. Its makers who become innovators. Its innovators who become inventors. For the world to move forward we need more makers.

Makeystreet

My first startup. With the internet levelling information, makers were getting more access to information, and the tools of production for making were becoming more and more democratised. Kickstarter was one such tool. Makeystreet was building another: a tool that made it easy for makers to find and discover parts, people and projects.

Tangle: the simplest earphone organizer

I ran a crowdfunding campaign myself to learn the details of how it works. It launched on Indiegogo, failed to meet the goal, but I learned a ton. Tangle was later sold online at getatangle.com.
